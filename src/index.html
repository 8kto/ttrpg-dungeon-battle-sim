How it works

Set up both sides, choose the settings and press Run. The simulator plays the battle many times and shows how often each side wins.

A battle

  • Each round a random side acts first, then the other
  • An attack hits when d20 + To Hit ≥ AC of the target
  • The battle ends when one side is wiped out, or in a draw if it never ends

Characters

AC Armor class, ascending: the higher, the harder to hit
To Hit Bonus added to the d20 attack roll
HD Hit dice: count and die type, e.g. 4 ✕ d8. Hit points are calculated by the strategy
Damage Attacks and their effects; click to edit. See
Saves Saving throws against special attacks: the save succeeds on d20 ≥ save value
  • ⎘ duplicates a row, ✕ removes it, Clear restores the defaults
  • Poisonous, Paralysis, Fiery, Petrifying and Breath give an extra attack to newly added monsters

Settings

Battles More battles give more stable results
Strategy How hit points and damage are calculated: Maximum takes max values, Average the average ones, Random rolls the dice. The same applies to effect damage and durations. Attack rolls and saves are always rolled
Rounding Whether Average rounds fractions up or down
Targeting Random: every attack picks a random enemy. Focus: an attacker sticks to one enemy until it dies or is petrified
Initiative Which side acts first: Random rolls it every round, Fixed once per battle, so the same side always goes first in that battle
Bias Chance (%) that a combatant of the side wastes its turn: tilts the balance without changing stats
Max Attacks per Target How many attacks one combatant can receive per round, as in a narrow corridor; 0 = unlimited
Print logs Show every roll; turn off for faster large runs

Results

  • Players win / Monsters win: share of battles won by each side
  • Avg Players survivors: share of players alive after a won battle; petrified ones don't count. A side whose combatants are all dead or petrified loses
  • Effect lines: how often each special effect hit or was saved against

Config

Export both sides and the settings as JSON to save or share a setup, and import it back.

Damage syntax

Attacks and options

  • d6, 2d4+1, d6+d10, d8-d4, 5 — damage formula: dice and numbers joined by + and -
  • , separates attacks made in one turn: d6, d6, d8 attacks three times
  • | separates alternative options, one option is used per turn: d6, d6 | d8 attacks twice with d6 or once with d8. The Max strategy picks the option with the highest max damage, Random and Average pick one at random

Names

  • A name in angle brackets after the formula labels the attack in the log: d6<Sword> | d4<Fire Breath>
  • Optional, up to 30 characters, without < > [ ] , |

Effects

An effect in square brackets after the formula and the name applies on hit: d6<Bite>[save, modifier, ...] or d6[modifier, ...]

  • A save (Paralyze, Poison, Breath, Device, Magic, in any case) goes first and gives the target a save; add a bonus or penalty like Poison(-2). A save needs modifiers
  • Without a save, modifiers always apply

Modifiers

Applied on a failed save or always without a save.

paralyzed
paralyzed(d4)		 The target skips its turns for the whole battle / for d4 turns; attacks against it always hit for max damage. Not to be confused with the Paralyze save: d6[Paralyze, paralyzed]
petrified
petrified(d4)		 The target turns to stone for the whole battle / for d4 turns: it can't act or be attacked and takes no damage over time; a save is optional, and an effect has at most one petrified. The other modifiers of the attack apply first, wherever petrified is written, and the other effects on the target wait until the petrification ends. A side whose combatants are all dead or petrified loses
save_or_die The target dies; requires a save
save_for_half(d8) d8 damage, half on a successful save; requires a save
d8 Extra d8 damage; requires a save (without a save, add it to the formula: d6+d8)
damage_each_round(d4)
damage_each_round(d4, 3)		 d4 damage every turn for the whole battle / for 3 turns
targets
targets(4)		 An area attack on 3 / 4 enemies (fewer if there are fewer); requires a save. There is no roll to hit: every target rolls the save and the damage on its own. On a failure it takes the damage of the attack and the modifiers, on a success nothing, only half of save_for_half. Each target uses one of its attacks per round

Examples

  • d6<Sword>[Poison, save_or_die]
  • d6[Paralyze(+2), paralyzed(d4)]
  • d4<Fire Breath>[Breath, save_for_half(2d6)]
  • d4<Gaze>[Paralyze, petrified]
  • d6<Bomb>[Paralyze, save_for_half(d4), targets(4)]
  • 0<Bomb>[Paralyze, save_for_half(d4), targets(4)]: a thrown bomb, only the effect deals damage
  • d4, d4 | d10<Fire>[damage_each_round(d4, 3)]
