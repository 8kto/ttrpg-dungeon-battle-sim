How it works

Set up both sides, choose the simulation settings and press Run. The simulator plays the battle many times and shows how often each side wins.

A battle

  • Each round both sides roll initiative: the winning side acts first, its members act in random order, then the other side does the same
  • On its turn a combatant makes all attacks of one damage option. An attack hits when d20 + To Hit ≥ AC of the target (ascending AC)
  • A combatant at 0 HP or less is dead and takes no further part in the battle
  • The battle ends when one side is wiped out; after 1000 rounds it is a draw (e.g. nobody can hit anybody)

Characters

AC Armor class, ascending: the higher, the harder to hit
To Hit Bonus added to the d20 attack roll
HD Hit dice: count and die type, e.g. 4 × d8. Hit points are calculated by the strategy
Damage Attacks, alternative options and their effects (poison, paralysis, fire...); click the chips to edit. See
Saves Saving throws vs paralysis, poison, breath, device and magic. A save succeeds when d20 + modifier ≥ save value; the modifier comes from the attack, e.g. poison(-2)
  • Add Player, Add Henchman and Add Monster add a character from a template; ⎘ duplicates a row, × removes it, Clear restores the default party or monsters
  • Poisonous, Paralysis and Fiery give an extra special attack to the monsters added afterwards; existing monsters are not changed

Simulation settings

Battles How many battles to play; more battles give more stable percentages
Strategy How hit points and damage are calculated: Maximum takes max values, Average the average ones, Random rolls the dice. Attack rolls and saves are always rolled
Rounding How the Average strategy rounds fractions, e.g. d8 = 4.5 becomes 5 (Up) or 4 (Down)
Targeting Random: every attack picks a random enemy. Focus: an attacker sticks to one enemy until one of them dies; if that enemy is out of attack slots this round (see below), the attack goes to someone else, but the focus stays
Player / Monster Bias Chance (%) that a combatant of the side wastes its turn: hesitation, a bad position, morale. Use it to tilt the balance without changing the stats
Max Attacks per Target How many attacks a single combatant can receive per round, as when only a few enemies can reach it in a corridor; 0 means unlimited
Print logs Log every roll of the first 500 battles; turn it off to run thousands of battles faster

Results

  • Players win / Monsters win: share of battles won by each side
  • Draws: battles stopped by the round limit
  • Avg Players survivors: how many of the players are still alive at the end of the battles they won
  • Effect lines: how many times each special effect was applied and how many times it was saved against
  • Avg Rounds: battle length
  • Stop interrupts the run; the summary covers the battles finished so far

Config

The Config tab exports both sides and the settings as JSON and imports them back: keep your setups in a file or share them.

Damage syntax

Attacks and options

  • d6, 2d4+1, d6+d4, 5 — damage formula
  • , separates attacks made in one turn: d6, d6, d8 attacks three times
  • | separates alternative options, one option is used per turn: d6, d6 | d8 attacks twice with d6 or once with d8. The Max strategy picks the option with the highest max damage, Random and Average pick one at random

Tags

Tags in brackets after a formula apply on hit: d6[tag, modifier, ...]

  • A tag without modifiers just labels the attack in the log: d6[knife] | d4[dagger]
  • A save name as the tag (paralyze, poison, breath, device, magic) makes the target roll that save once per attack; a bonus or penalty goes in parentheses with a sign: poison(-2), paralyze(+2)
  • Any other tag (fire, 🧊) has no save: modifiers always apply

Modifiers

Applied on a failed save or when the tag has no save.

paralyze
paralyze(d4)		 The target skips its turns until the end of battle / for d4 turns; attacks against it hit automatically for max weapon damage, and it fails all saves
save_or_die The target dies; requires a save tag
save_for_half(d8) d8 damage, half (rounded down) on a successful save; requires a save tag
d8 Extra d8 damage
damage_each_round(d4)
damage_each_round(d4, 3)		 d4 damage at the start of each of the target's turns from the next round, until the end of battle / for 3 turns; identical effects don't stack

Examples

  • d6[poison, save_or_die]
  • d6[paralyze(+2), paralyze(d4)]
  • d6[breath, save_for_half(2d6)]
  • d4, d4 | d10[fire, damage_each_round(d4, 3)]
